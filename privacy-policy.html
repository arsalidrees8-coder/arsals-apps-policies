Privacy Policy — Cleaner Book

Developer: ARSAL'S APPS
App: Cleaner Book (com.cleanerbook.app), version 1.0.8
Effective date: 14 November 2026
Support: cleanerbooksupport@gmail.com

Short version: your files never leave your phone. Scanning and cleaning happen only on your device. Only tips and ads use the internet.

No account, no login

No account or login is needed. We do not know who you are, and we hold no profile, name, email address or contact details for you.

Your files stay on your device

Scanning, duplicate detection, cleaning, Trash and history all happen on this phone. The only network uses are ads and device tips. No photo, file, name, contact or location is sent anywhere.

Duplicates are exact byte-identical copies only. Similar photos are near-identical by a strict on-device image hash and must be previewed. Blurry photos are scored on-device and must be previewed. Nothing is deleted without your preview and confirmation: items go to on-device Trash first, where you can restore them, or are deleted permanently only after you confirm.

What the app does on your device

  • Scans storage for junk files, large files, duplicate, similar and blurry photos, screenshots, leftover APK files and empty folders.
  • Shows per-app cache sizes and last-used dates only after you grant usage access in system Settings; that information never leaves your phone.
  • Shows battery information and a storage breakdown from real measured values. The app never invents per-app battery percentages and never claims to improve battery health, speed or cooling.
  • Keeps your cleaning history and Trash on your device only.

Network uses

  • Ads: the app shows ads from AdMob — a banner and a full-screen ad after a scan. Ads from AdMob may use device or advertising identifiers to show ads. Nothing is sold. Ads never block a feature: if no ad is available, everything still works.
  • Device tips: a small text file of device tips loads online and refreshes when you are connected. No file is ever uploaded.

Permissions — and why

  • Internet, Network state — AdMob ads and online device tips only.
  • Notifications — scan-complete notices, dismissible by swiping.
  • Photos, videos and audio (Android 13+) / Storage (up to Android 12) — photo, video and audio scanning.
  • All files access (optional) — full junk, large-file and APK scanning. The app works in a limited mode without it and says so clearly.
  • Installed apps list — App Manager only, so installed apps can be reviewed and uninstalled through the system prompt. The app never silently uninstalls another app.
  • Usage access (optional, granted by you in system Settings) — per-app cache sizes and last-used dates only.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. There is no chat, no sharing with strangers and no in-app purchase. Everything in the app is free.

Data retention and deletion

Your files, history and Trash live only on your device. Delete them inside the app or by uninstalling the app. We hold no copy anywhere, so there is nothing to ask us to delete.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version appears here and in the app. Contact the developer at cleanerbooksupport@gmail.com.
